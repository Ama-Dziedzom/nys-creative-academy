About NYS

Built in Akuapem.
Made for the world.

We are investing in the people who will shape what comes next: the young, creative and ambitious minds of Abotakyi and its neighbouring towns.

The initiative

Empowering Akuapem's youth for the global stage.

The NYS Creative Academy was born out of a simple, powerful vision: to transform the local economy of Abotakyi and other towns in Akuapem by investing in our greatest resource—the youth.

By providing intensive, hands-on training in Digital Marketing, AI, Filmmaking, Writing and Music Production, we are building pathways to sustainable, independent incomes.

Founder

Nana Yaw Sakyi

A legacy project

Leadership rooted in the future.

The Academy marks the one-year anniversary of Nana Yaw Sakyi's enstoolment as Apesemakahene of Abotakyi Akuapem. Rather than a traditional celebration, he chose to invest this milestone into a legacy project that directly impacts his people.
